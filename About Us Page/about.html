About SpoilSafe

Building a world without hidden spoilage

SpoilSafe was started by Carnegie Mellon makers who saw kitchens, retailers, and distributors losing food because freshness data was trapped in storage rooms. Today we help teams see the earliest signs of change so they can safeguard quality, margins, and trust.

Our Story

Born in the kitchens of Carnegie Mellon

We witnessed chefs and operators throwing away pallets because no one could see what was happening inside cold rooms. SpoilSafe combines sensing, analytics, and gentle automation so operators can intervene early rather than write off product late.

Recognising the waste

450 million tons of food vanish across retail and hospitality before a customer ever sees it. Little changes in temperature, humidity, or ethylene stack up silently.

Designing with teams

We co-create with chefs, facility managers, and supply leads to ensure data is trustworthy, actionable, and simple to share across locations.

Proving the impact

Every deployment is paired with clear baselines so partners can see reductions in waste, labour hours saved, and fresher product on shelves.
